## Ensemble Experience

- **Orchestra** — violist, 2017–2021. I was the viola section leader of my school's top string orchestra.
- **Jazz Ensemble** — pianist, 2018–2021. I was the pianist for my school's top jazz ensemble.
- **Piano Trio** — pianist, 2019–2021. I was the pianist for a self-assembled piano trio ensemble; we played the first movement of Saint-Saëns' *Piano Trio No. 2*, the first movement of Faure's *Piano Trio*, Rachmaninoff's *Trio élégiaque No. 1*, Rachmaninoff's *Trio élégiaque No. 2*, and the third movement of Ravel's *Piano Trio*.
- **Illinois Music Education Association** — district and state violist, 2019–2020. I was a violist for the Illinois Music Education Association district and state festival orchestra for the 2019-2020 school year. At the district level, we played Khachaturian's *Masquerade* and Dvorak's *Carnival Overture*, and at the state level, we played the second movement of Shostakovich's *Symphony No. 10 in E minor* and Tchaikovsky's *1812 Overture*.
- **String Quartet** — violist, 2020. I was the violist for a string quartet; we played the first and second movement of Borodin's *String Quartet No. 2*.
- **Piano Quintet** — piano, 2021–2022. I was the pianist for a piano quintet as part of MIT's chamber music program; we played Brahms' *Piano Quintet in F minor* and Dvorak's *Piano Quintet in A major*.
- **Accompanist** — for various students, 2018–current. I've accompanied the first movement of Beethoven's *Violin Sonata No. 5*, Ravel's *Tzigane*, the second movement of Franck's *Violin Sonata*, the first movement of Lalo's *Cello Concerto*, Saint-Saëns' *Le cygne*, Enescu's *Cantabile et presto*, the first movement of Poulenc's *Flute Sonata*, Ganne's *Andante et Scherzo*, Taffanel's *Andante Pastoral et Scherzettino*, Gaubert's *Fantaisie*, Beethoven's *Violin Sonata No. 1*, Fauré's *Violin Sonata No. 1*, the first movement of Rachmaninoff's *Piano Concerto No. 1*, Mozart's *Violin Sonata No. 18*, Bach's *Flute Sonata in E Major*, Prokofiev's *Flute Sonata*, Sancan's *Sonatine for flute and piano*, Hindemith's *Der Schwanendreher*, Faure's *Berceuse*, and Bowen's *Viola Sonata No. 1*.
- **Orchestra** — violist, 2022–ongoing. I am a violist in the MIT Symphony Orchestra. We played Britten's *Double Concerto for Violin and Viola*, Haydn's *Symphony No. 88*, Mayer's *Symphony No. 5*, Price's *Dances in the Canebrakes*, Korngold's *Cello Concerto*, Mussorgsky's *Pictures at an Exhibition* (orch. Ravel), Clara Schumann's *Piano Concerto*, Bartók's *Duke Bluebeard's Castle*, Romero's *Fuja con Pajarillo*, Levin's *Espressões*, Ginastera's *Suite from Estancia*, Piazzolla's *Tangazo*, Frank's *Three Latin American Dances*, Bonds' *Montgomery Variations*, Dvořák's *Symphony No. 7*, Price's *Symphony No. 3*, the first movement of Rachmaninoff's *Piano Concerto No. 1*, Bruckner's *Symphony No. 4*, Brahms' *Academic Festival Overture*, and Mozart's *Requiem* (on portative organ).
