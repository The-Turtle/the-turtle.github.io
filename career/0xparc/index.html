0xPARC

I spent one year at 0xPARC on a team of four building a performant, production-grade fully homomorphic encryption library from the ground up — no dependencies (except for one GPU). Here's why we built it, how we built it, and what we learned along the way.

These posts are written for a wide audience. Depending on what you're here for, different parts will be more useful:

  • If you just want to know what I've been up to or what FHE is at a high level, Part 1 is for you.
  • If you want to use our system to build something, Part 2 covers the interface, cost model, and worked examples.
  • If you want to know every last detail of how FHE works at a technical level, Part 3 goes under the hood.
  • If you're a security engineer wondering whether our system is secure, Part 4 covers the threat models and known attacks.
